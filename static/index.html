CONSOLE OPTIONS
What this console shows you


      

        
Status cards

        
Tip: you can also drag a card straight from the summary strip up into the hero row to pin it, drag one card onto another to reorder, or drag a hero card down to the strip to demote it. Your arrangement is saved. The checkboxes below do the same thing from the keyboard.

        
Why does the hero row change on its own? With focus mode on, any card that turns red or amber is raised into the hero row automatically and drops back out when it clears — those are marked with a dashed edge. Cards you pinned or demoted never move. Turn focus mode off below for a layout that only ever changes when you change it.

        
There are 17 status cards. Most read nominal most of the time, which buries the one or two that don't. Focus mode keeps exceptions full-size and folds the healthy ones into a single line — nothing is hidden, it just stops competing.
